# Cheap CH341A Programmer 3.3V Safe Wiring & Pinout Guide
- **CH341A 3.3V Mod:** Lift Pin 28 (VCC) on the CH341A IC and jumper it to the 3.3V output pin of the AMS1117-3.3V regulator (and Pin 9 V3) so both VCC and SPI/I2C data lines run at true 3.3V instead of 5V.
- **In-Circuit Automotive Tip:** When clipping onto a 95xxx/24Cxx chip on a vehicle circuit board at 3.3V, bridge the board's main crystal (XTAL) pins to hold the vehicle MCU in reset so it does not interfere on the bus.
- **8-Pin SOIC8 Pinout (Red Stripe = Pin 1):**
  - 95xxx/25xxx SPI: 1=/CS, 2=MISO(DO), 3=/WP(+3.3V), 4=GND, 5=MOSI(DI), 6=CLK, 7=/HOLD(+3.3V), 8=VCC(+3.3V)
  - 24Cxx I2C: 1=A0(GND), 2=A1(GND), 3=A2(GND), 4=GND, 5=SDA, 6=SCL, 7=WP(GND to write), 8=VCC(+3.3V)
