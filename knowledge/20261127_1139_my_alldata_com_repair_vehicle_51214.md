### Rules

- Ohm's Law: V = IR
- Kirchhoff's Current Law: I = I1 + I2 + ... + In
- Kirchhoff's Voltage Law: V = V1 + V2 + ... + Vn
- Thevenin's Theorem: Vth = (Vt / Zt) * (Rt || Zt)
- Norton's Theorem: Vn = (I_n * R_n) || (Vn / Zn)
